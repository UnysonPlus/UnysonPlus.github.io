# Image Scroll Choreography

Pin foreground **images** to the screen and choreograph each one to the page scroll: it **appears**, **holds** on its corner while the visitor scrolls through a whole "chapter", then **exits**, all at the scroll percentages you choose. Several images share one scroll timeline in a click-through layer your sections scroll underneath.

Builder palette → **Animation Engine** → **Image Scroll Choreography**. Its typographic sibling is [Text Scroll Choreography](/animation-engine/text-scroll-choreography), and both share the [Live Editor & Keyframe Timeline](/shortcodes/animation-elements/scroll-choreography-editor).
