# Text Scroll Choreography

Pin foreground **text** to the screen and choreograph each phrase to the page scroll: it **appears**, **holds** on its corner while the visitor reads a whole "chapter", then **exits**, all at the scroll percentages you choose. Every line can **tilt**, **3D-warp** or **curve onto an arc**, and play one of the Animation Engine's [Text Effects](/animation-engine/text-effects) on its letters, while staying real, selectable text.

Builder palette → **Animation Engine** → **Text Scroll Choreography**. Its picture sibling is [Image Scroll Choreography](/animation-engine/image-scroll-choreography), and both share the [Live Editor & Keyframe Timeline](/shortcodes/animation-elements/scroll-choreography-editor).
