The **Animation Engine** is a bundled Unyson+ extension that turns the page builder into a full motion platform — scroll-driven animation, hover interactions, physics, a custom cursor, animated backgrounds, kinetic text, page transitions, and real-time 3D/WebGL/SVG elements. No code.

:::info[It ships **off** by default]
The engine is included with Unyson+ but **inactive** until you switch it on: **WP Admin → UnysonPlus → Extensions → Animation Engine → Activate**. Keeping it off means none of its assets or options exist on sites that don't need them.

The exceptions are [Entrance Animation](/animation-engine/entrance-animation) (Animate.css) and [Exit Animation](/animation-engine/exit-animation): they are part of core and always available, engine or not.
:::

## What it adds

Once active, the engine adds effects in four places.

:::tip[Adding animations — the inserter]
The **Animations** tab is an **Add Animation** inserter. It starts empty with a **+ Add Animation** button: click it to browse a searchable grid of effects in five categories (**Entrance**, **Exit**, **Scroll**, **Interaction**, **Ambient**) and pick one. It drops in as a configurable **card**, and you can **stack several** on one element (an Entrance *and* a Hover *and* a Scroll effect). Some modules can be added **more than once** to combine their effects (see below). Remove a card with its **×**. Effects you haven't added take no space, so the tab stays tidy however many modules are installed.
:::

## Combining & stacking effects

There are two different ways to layer motion on one element:

**1. Combine *different* modules** — always available. Any element can carry several effects from different modules at once, each its own card: e.g. an **Entrance** animation *and* a **Hover** interaction *and* a **Scroll Reveal**. Just add each from the inserter.

**2. Stack *the same* module** — add one module more than once to combine its own effects. Its inserter tile stays available after you add it, so clicking it again drops another card. This only exists where the effects genuinely layer without fighting:

| Module | Stackable? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hover Interaction** | ✅ Up to 2 per element | Combine independent effects — e.g. **Lift + Ripple**. Two *motion* effects (Magnetic / 3D Tilt / Lift) fight over the element's transform, so pick one of those plus a decoration (Glow, Spotlight, Ripple, Underline, Color Shift, Image Reveal, Scramble). |
| **Interactions** | ✅ Up to 2 per element | E.g. a hover *and* a click on the same element. |
| **Background Effect** | ✅ Up to 12 layers | Layer several animated backgrounds behind one container. |
| Entrance, Exit, Scroll Effect, Physics, Parallax, Marquee, Text Effect, Scroll Reveal, 3D Flip Card, States | ❌ One per element | A second instance would fight the first over the same CSS property or rebuild the same content, so these are single by design — their tile leaves the grid once added. |
| Card Stack, Horizontal Scroll, Infinite Scroll Loop, Scrollytelling, Motion Sequence | ❌ One per Section | Section-level — each arranges the whole section, so only one is possible. |

:::note[Why not stack everything?]
Stacking is offered only where it's genuinely useful. Forcing it on effects that share the same `transform`/`clip-path` or rebuild the same DOM would just make them clobber each other — so those stay one-per-element on purpose.
:::

## Performance & accessibility

The engine is built to stay out of the way:

- **Loads only where used.** Every effect's CSS/JS is enqueued **only on pages that actually use it** — a page with no effects ships none of these bytes. Heavy libraries (GSAP, Three.js, model-viewer) are vendored and load only for the pages that need them.
- **Reduced motion.** Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, gated by the engine-wide **Respect "reduce motion"** setting (Theme Settings → Site-wide UX → Animation Engine, on by default). Effects fall back to a static frame or plain content.
- **Touch aware.** Pointer-driven effects (Hover, Cursor, pointer-following Physics) are skipped on touch screens, and the engine-wide **Disable heavy effects on mobile** setting skips the GPU-heavy ones (WebGL and physics) on phones.
- **Off-screen pause.** Continuous effects (backgrounds, ambient physics) pause when scrolled out of view and when the tab is hidden.
- **Editor-safe & flash-free.** In the builder and the Live Editor's Edit mode, effects hold still at their final state, and guards prevent a flash of un-animated content.

:::tip[Control lives in the inserter]
Effects are added **per element** from the **Add Animation** inserter — there's no global on/off panel to keep in sync, and nothing loads on a page that doesn't use it. **Theme Settings → Site-wide UX** holds the engine-wide **Animation Engine** sub-tab (**Respect "reduce motion"**, **Disable heavy effects on mobile**) plus the site-wide features: **Preloader**, **Cursor**, **Interactions**, **Page Transitions**, **Smooth Scroll**, **Viewport Units** and **Scroll Progress**.
:::

:::tip[Build your own]
The engine is extensible — add your **own effect or module** from your child theme's `functions.php` and it shows up in the inserter automatically. See **[Add your own module / effect](./custom-modules.md)**.
:::
