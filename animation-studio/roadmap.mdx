# Roadmap

Seven phases, built in order. Phases 1 to 3 make a complete hand-built studio: design a hero animation on
the timeline and export it as HTML. Phases 1 and 2 are built and live at
[animation-studio.unysonplus.com](https://animation-studio.unysonplus.com/). Phase 4 puts scenes into Unyson+ pages, phase 5 adds the AI, and the
later phases add 3D, finer control and video export. Every phase builds on the same scene file, so nothing
made early has to be redone later.

## Order of work

1. **Foundations.** The scene file, the player and the responsive stage come first, because everything
   else reads or writes them. A hand-written scene playing on the live subdomain is the first milestone.
2. **Editor.** The canvas, the timeline, the inspector and about twenty presets: enough to build a real
   hero by hand.
3. **Export.** Standalone HTML and an embed snippet, each recording the player version it was made with.
4. **Unyson+ integration.** A page-builder element with an **Open in Studio** round trip, and one preset
   library shared with the [Animation Engine](/animation-engine).
5. **AI.** Prompts produce scene files through the [AI Dev Kit](/ai-dev-kit), so every result can be edited
   on the timeline.
6. **3D and finer control.** three.js clips, a keyframe and curve editor, and scroll-linked playback.
7. **Later.** Video export, accounts with cloud saving, and click-to-advance presentations.

The plan and the reasons behind it are on the [overview](./index.md); what already exists elsewhere is on the
[research](./research.md) page.
