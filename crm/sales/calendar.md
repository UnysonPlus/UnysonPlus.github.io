# Calendar

The calendar puts everything with a date on one screen: your tasks, calls and meetings by their time, deals
on the day they are expected to close, and email campaigns on the day they are scheduled or were sent.

The calendar is a module: switch it on under [CRM → Modules](../workspace/settings-and-modules.md).

<img src="/img/crm/calendar.png" alt="The WordPress CRM calendar month view with tasks, meetings and deal close dates" width="1920" />

## Month and week

- **Month** shows up to three items a day, with "N more" linking to that week.
- **Week** shows every item with its details: type, duration, place and the record it belongs to.
- **Previous**, **Today** and **Next** move through time; a day's number opens its week.

<img src="/img/crm/calendar-week.png" alt="The WordPress CRM calendar week view with details for each task and meeting" width="1920" />

Overdue items show in red and done ones are struck through. Click any item to open its contact, company,
deal or campaign.

## Whose calendar

You see your own tasks and deals. Managers can switch to anyone's calendar, or everyone's. Campaigns show for
managers.

## Add a task from the calendar

Hover a day and click **+** to add a task for that day (09:00 by default, or the next hour today). To put the
task on a contact, company or deal, add it from that record's page instead.

## On a phone

The month becomes a list of the days that have something on.

## For other extensions

Extensions can add their own items to the calendar, such as membership renewals. See
[PHP API and hooks](../developers/php-api-and-hooks.md).
