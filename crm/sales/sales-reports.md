# Sales reports

**CRM → Reports** turns your deals into answers: how much is in the pipeline, what is likely to close and when,
how many deals you win, and why you lose the others.

<img src="/img/crm/reports.png" alt="Sales reports in the WordPress CRM: headline figures, deals by stage, forecast, won and lost per month, lost reasons and performance by owner" width="1920" />

## Filters

- **Pipeline**: one pipeline or all of them.
- **Period**: this month, the last 3, 6 or 12 months, this year, or custom dates. The period decides which
  **closed** deals count; the open pipeline and the forecast are always as of today.
- **Owner** (managers): everyone, or one person.

## What the report shows

| Section | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Open pipeline** | The value and number of open deals |
| **Weighted forecast** | Open value × each stage's probability: what is realistically likely to close |
| **Won** | Value and number of deals won in the period |
| **Win rate** | Won ÷ (won + lost) in the period |
| **Average deal won** | The typical size of a win, and how many days deals take from created to won |
| **Open deals by stage** | Where the open value sits, with the weighted part shown solid |
| **Forecast by expected close** | Open deals by the month they should close, plus those past their date and those with no date |
| **Won and lost** | Won and lost value per month (with a table of the numbers) |
| **Why deals were lost** | The lost reasons, most common first |
| **By owner** | Each person's open pipeline, weighted value, wins, win rate and days to close (managers) |

Sales reps see their own numbers. Deals in the bin are left out.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is the weighted forecast lower than the open pipeline?**
It counts each deal at its chance of closing. A deal in an early stage counts for a small part of its value.

**How do I improve the forecast?**
Keep expected close dates and stages up to date, and set realistic probabilities on your stages under
**Deals → Edit pipelines**.

**What about deals in different currencies?**
Amounts are added up as stored and shown in the pipeline's currency; they are not converted.
