# Tasks, calls and meetings

Tasks keep follow-ups from slipping. A task is a **to-do**, a **call**, a **meeting** or an **email** with a due
time and an owner, on a contact, a company or a deal (or on nothing, as a personal to-do).

## Your tasks

**CRM → Tasks** shows your tasks grouped as **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming** and **Done recently**. A
manager can switch to anyone's tasks, or everyone's. Home shows a **Your tasks** card with what is overdue
and due today.

## Add a task

On any contact, company or deal page, open **Tasks → Add a task**:

1. Choose the **type** and the **due** date and time.
2. Say **what** it is, such as "Call about the quote".
3. For a call or meeting, set **how long** it should take; for a meeting, **where** and **with whom** (the
   contact, a company's people or a deal's contacts).
4. Managers can assign it to someone else with **For**.

You can also add a task from the [calendar](./calendar.md) by clicking + on a day.

## Reminders

An hourly job emails each person one reminder listing their tasks that are due within the hour or overdue.
Each task is reminded about once; changing its due time earns a new reminder.

## Ticking off a task

Click the circle next to a task to mark it done. For a **call or meeting**, the CRM asks **how it went** (an
outcome) and **how long** it took. The entry lands on the record's timeline, such as "Meeting done: Quarterly
review (45 min)", and on each attendee's timeline.

## Log a call or meeting that already happened

Use **Tasks → Log a call or meeting** on a record: choose call or meeting, when it happened, an optional
subject, the outcome and how long it took. It is saved as done and logged on the timeline straight away.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who gets reminders?**
The task's owner, by email, through your site's mail set-up.

**Can a sales rep give tasks to others?**
No. A rep's tasks are their own; managers can assign tasks to anyone.

**Can I see tasks on a calendar?**
Yes. See [Calendar](./calendar.md).
