# Home dashboard

**CRM → Home** shows where things stand the moment you open the CRM.

<img src="/img/crm/home.png" alt="The CRM Home dashboard with counts, contacts by stage, the pipeline funnel, recent activity and tasks" width="1920" />

- **The status line and counts**: contacts and how many are new this month, companies, people subscribed to
  your emails and customers. Each count opens the matching list.
- **Contacts by stage**: how many contacts are subscribers, leads, opportunities, customers and inactive.
- **Pipeline**: open deal value per stage of your default pipeline, with the weighted total.
- **Recent activity**: what happened across every record lately.
- **Your tasks**: what is overdue and due today.
- **Newest contacts** and **Email campaigns**.

A sales rep's Home is about their own records. An empty CRM shows the three ways contacts arrive instead.

## Customise Home

Everyone can arrange their own Home. Click **Customise Home**:

<img src="/img/crm/home-customise.png" alt="Customising the CRM Home: move, hide and drag cards between the two columns" width="1920" />

- **Drag** a card by its handle, within a column or to the other column.
- Or use the buttons on each card: **move up**, **move down**, **move to the other column**, **hide**.
- Hidden cards wait in the tray at the top; **Show** puts one back.
- **Reset to the default layout** undoes it all.

Changes save as you make them and only change your own Home. Cards added by other extensions (such as a
Membership card) join the same layout.
