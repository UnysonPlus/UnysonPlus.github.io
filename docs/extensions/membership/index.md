# Membership

:::info Coming soon
The Membership extension is planned. This page will cover it once work begins.
:::

Membership will open pages, posts and page sections to the right people, and connect to the
[CRM](../crm/index.md) so access can follow a contact's tags and lists, and membership activity can show
up on the contact's timeline and in automations.

Follow progress in the [CRM roadmap](../crm/roadmap.mdx), whose integrations phase prepares the
connection Membership will use.
