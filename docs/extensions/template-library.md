# Template Library

The **Template Library** is a catalog of ready-made layouts for the page builder: **full starter pages** (a whole home
page, with its photos) and **sections** (a FAQ, testimonials, a stats band…). Pick one, click **Install** and
**Insert**, and it lands on your page as ordinary builder sections and elements: change the words, swap the pictures,
move things around. Nothing stays locked to the template.

It is how a new site gets from an empty page to a finished-looking home page in a minute, and it is free.

## Turn it on

**Unyson+ → Extensions → Template Library → Activate.** It needs the page builder, which it switches on with it.

## Use a template

1. Open a page with the page builder (**Pages → Add New**, or an existing page).
2. Click **Template Library** in the builder's toolbar (next to *Insert Section*).
3. Choose a tab: **All**, **Full pages**, **Sections** or **Columns**, or search and filter by category.
4. A template you have not used yet shows **Install**: click it. It downloads in a few seconds and the button becomes
   **Insert**.
5. Click **Insert**. It is added to the end of the page; tick **Replace page instead of appending** first to start the
   page over with it.
6. **Publish** (or **Update**) the page.

Click a thumbnail to see it larger. **My Templates** lists the sections and pages you saved yourself (*Save as Template*
in the builder).

## The starter pages

| Starter page | What it has |
|---|---|
| **Café or bakery** | A welcome with two buttons, three reasons to visit, your story, three menu favourites with prices, guest reviews, and opening hours with the address, phone and email. |
| **Small business** | For a consultant, agency or local service: what you do, three services, about you, a three-step "how it works", client reviews, a call to action, and contact details with office hours. |
| **Portfolio** | For a photographer, designer or other creative: an introduction, a six-picture grid of your work, what you offer, about you, kind words from clients and a way to book you. |

Each one is written as a real page (headings, short paragraphs, buttons that jump to the right section), so it reads well
before you change a word, with clear places to put your own text.

**They take your site's look.** The starters use your theme's colours, fonts and button styles (Theme Settings), not
colours of their own, so changing your colours or fonts once restyles the whole page.

## Pictures come with them

A starter page's photos are installed with it, into **your Media Library**, and the page uses those copies:

- They are on your own site, so nothing loads from another server.
- They appear in **Media** like any picture you uploaded, titled after the template ("Café or bakery template").
  Replace one on the page (click the image element → choose another), or edit it in the Media Library.
- Installing the same template again, or another template that uses the same photo, reuses the picture already there
  instead of adding a second copy.

The photos are free to use (CC0, public domain), so you can keep them on a live site or replace them with your own.

## Sections

Sections drop into any page between your own content.

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **FAQ Accordion** | Five common questions in an accordion, ready for your answers. |
| **Image Showcase** | A picture beside a headline, a few lines and a button. |
| **Testimonials** | Customer quotes in a slider. |
| **Stats Band** / **Counters Row** | Animated numbers with captions. |

## Good to know

- **Installed templates are kept in** `wp-content/uploads/unysonplus/templates/`, so the plugin stays small and an
  update never removes them.
- **Inserting copies the layout.** Later changes to a template never change pages you already made with it. For a
  section that updates everywhere it is used, use a [Global Template](./global-templates.md).
- **The catalog is read from the Unyson+ library on GitHub** and refreshed every 12 hours. If your server cannot reach
  it, the Library shows only the templates already installed.
- Starter pages are best on a new page (or with **Replace page**): they are a whole page, from the welcome to the
  contact details.
