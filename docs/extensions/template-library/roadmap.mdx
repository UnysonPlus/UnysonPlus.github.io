# Roadmap

Next for the Template Library: **Starter Sites**, a free import and export for whole small websites
(pages, the look, header and footer, menus, forms and photos in one package). It is the modern successor
of the original Unyson demo importer: instead of restoring a full database backup that replaces the
site, it imports a light package and lets you choose what to bring over, with undo. The same export makes
packages of your own sites, to move a design between sites or hand it to a client.

Alongside it, a store will sell ready-made starter sites and landing page templates: each kit bought once
and downloaded forever (no subscriptions), imported with Starter Sites and made your own by hand or with the
AI Assistant. People browse and preview the kits on **build.unysonplus.com** (the storefront) and buy and
download them on **www.unysonplus.com** (the shop, with WooCommerce); each kit has a live preview on its own
demos.unysonplus.com subsite. **Unyson+ itself stays completely free**: no license key, no locked items and
no shop inside the plugin.

The why is in the Design Decisions log:
[Why sell landing page templates instead of prompts?](/decisions/sell-templates-not-prompts)
Starter Sites tasks are checked against the extension's files automatically; store and content tasks are
updated by hand.
