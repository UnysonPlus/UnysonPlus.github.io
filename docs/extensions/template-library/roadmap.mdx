# Roadmap

Unyson+ stays free. Next for the Template Library: **premium landing page templates**, sold at
**build.unysonplus.com** and installed from wp-admin with a license key. A buyer gets a finished, tested
page that looks exactly like its preview, then makes it their own in the page builder or by telling the
AI Assistant what to change. Free templates stay in every site's library; Pro ones show locked until a
key is entered, and keep working after a plan ends (only new installs and updates stop).

Why templates rather than prompts is recorded in the Design Decisions log:
[Why sell landing page templates instead of prompts?](/decisions/sell-templates-not-prompts)
Template Library tasks are checked against the extension's files automatically; store and content tasks
are updated by hand.
