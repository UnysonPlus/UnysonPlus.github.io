# Build a free WordPress site fast (from a design or an AI builder)

You don't have to build a WordPress site from a blank page. **Design or generate the look in whatever
tool you're fastest in** — a design app like Figma, an AI site builder, or even a single prompt — then
bring it into WordPress **for free** with the Unyson+ **[Site Converter](/extensions/site-converter)**.
You end up with a real, self-hosted WordPress site you **own and can edit**, with no monthly platform
fee and no lock-in.

## Two ways in

1. **From a live URL** — if your tool publishes a site (most AI website builders do), convert straight
   from its URL.
2. **From exported HTML/CSS** — if your tool gives you code or a design export (a design app, or an AI
   that outputs HTML), convert the file. The upload path is covered in the
   [AI-generated HTML guide](/guides/convert-ai-generated-html-to-wordpress).

Either way the result is native, editable [page-builder](/page-builder) pages plus a matching theme.

## The fast, free workflow

1. **Make the design** in your tool of choice.
2. **Install [Unyson+](/installation)** (free) and activate **Site Converter** under *Unyson+ →
   Extensions*.
3. **Convert** — [from a URL](/extensions/site-converter/convert-from-url), or from your HTML/CSS
   export.
4. **Edit** in the builder and add what only WordPress gives you: posts, forms, WooCommerce, SEO.
5. **Publish** on hosting you choose.

:::tip[Not close enough? Let an AI agent finish the job]
An automatic conversion is a **close, editable starting point — not always pixel-perfect.** To get it
the rest of the way, hand it to an AI agent like **Claude**: it runs the conversion, compares the result
to your original section by section, and refines it until it matches. See
**[Convert a site to WordPress with an AI agent](/guides/convert-site-to-wordpress-with-ai)**.
:::
