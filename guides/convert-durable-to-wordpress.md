# How to move a Durable site to WordPress (free)

**Durable** spins up a site from a prompt in seconds, but it's a **hosted, subscription platform** —
your site, CMS and hosting all live inside Durable. Moving to **WordPress** gives you a site you fully
own: a real CMS, thousands of plugins, your own SEO and forms, and no ongoing platform fee. The free
**[Site Converter](/extensions/site-converter)** rebuilds your design into native, editable
page-builder pages.

Because a Durable site renders in the browser, the route is to **convert from its published URL**.

## Step 1 — Get your live Durable URL

Make sure your Durable site is **published** and publicly reachable — on its Durable address or your
connected custom domain.

## Step 2 — Install Unyson+ and Site Converter

1. Install and activate **[Unyson+](/installation)** (free).
2. **Unyson+ → Extensions** → activate **Site Converter**.
3. Start the **[capture service](/extensions/site-converter/capture-service)** — for a URL conversion
   this renders the page in Chrome and captures the real colors, fonts and layout. (One-time local
   setup.)

## Step 3 — Convert from the URL

1. **Unyson+ → Convert** → **Convert** tab → **From a URL**.
2. Paste your **live Durable URL**.
3. Set options — **Create child theme**, **Capture header/footer**, **Import images**.
4. Click **Convert to WordPress**, or **Review mapping first**, then **Build the site**.

Full walkthrough: **[Convert from a URL](/extensions/site-converter/convert-from-url)**.

## Step 4 — Edit in the builder, then drop the subscription

Every page comes across **fully editable** in the [page builder](/page-builder). Once you're on
WordPress you can add real SEO plugins, unlimited forms, WooCommerce and custom post types — and stop
paying Durable's monthly fee.

:::tip[Not close enough? Let an AI agent finish the job]
An automatic conversion is a **close, editable starting point — not always pixel-perfect.** To get it
the rest of the way, hand it to an AI agent like **Claude**: it runs the conversion, compares the result
to your original section by section, and refines it until it matches. See
**[Convert a site to WordPress with an AI agent](/guides/convert-site-to-wordpress-with-ai)**.
:::

## FAQ

**Is it free?** Yes — Unyson+ and the Site Converter are free, with no pro tier. You provide your own
hosting and domain.

**Will my content come over?** The page design and content are rebuilt from the live page; dynamic
features are re-added with WordPress plugins, which is where they belong.
