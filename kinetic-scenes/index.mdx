Kinetic Scenes is a browser editor for motion graphics: big kinetic type, shapes, pictures, footage, code, diagrams and 3D,
laid out on a timeline the way a video editor lays out clips. You drag a preset onto the timeline, change its words,
picture or timing, and watch it play. Then you choose where it goes:

- **On a web page**, as **plain HTML**. The scene plays live in the page, stays sharp at any size, weighs kilobytes instead
  of megabytes, keeps its words readable by search engines and screen readers, and can play on load, as it scrolls into
  view, in step with the scroll, or slide by slide on click.
- **As a video**, for Reels, TikTok, Shorts, YouTube or anywhere HTML can't go: MP4, WebM or ProRes, in one format or all
  of them at once, with sound, a cover image and, if you like, a see-through background to lay over your own footage.

## Why Kinetic Scenes

Most AI video tools and open-source video projects make one of two things: a finished video file from a prompt, or a
video written as code that a developer renders on a server. Kinetic Scenes sits between them and does what neither does.

- **It makes a web page, not only a video.** A scene plays live in the browser: sharp at any size, a few kilobytes
  instead of megabytes, with real text that search engines, screen readers and translation tools can read. When you do
  need a video, the same scene renders to MP4, WebM or ProRes in every social format at once.
- **You edit it on a timeline.** Layers, clips, eases, a camera and sound, the way a motion designer works. A prompt-only
  tool gives you a result you can only re-roll; a code-only project needs a developer for every change. Here you drag,
  trim and retype, and the scene file stays readable for code and AI alike.
- **AI is a helper, not a requirement.** AI assist plans a scene, builds it with real presets, looks at the frames and
  fixes what it sees, with every change a single undo step. It can run on your own computer through the AI Dev Kit, and
  everything else works with no AI at all.
- **Every frame is the same everywhere.** Each frame is worked out from the time alone, so the Studio preview, your web
  page and the exported video match exactly, and a scene can play on load, in view, with the scroll or slide by slide.
- **Motion that looks designed.** 87 presets and about 45 video editing effects, with eases measured from professional
  motion design, plus a Check scene that flags text too small or too quick to read before anyone else sees it.
- **Nothing to set up.** It runs in the browser with no account and no server, and drops into WordPress as the Kinetic
  Scene element, with its presets also usable on ordinary page elements ([Presets on your pages](./on-your-pages.md)).
- **Kind to visitors.** Scenes respect the reduced-motion setting and show a still frame instead.

## Why HTML instead of video, for the web

- **Small.** A scene is a few kilobytes of data plus a shared player, not a multi-megabyte video file.
- **Sharp everywhere.** Text and shapes are drawn by the browser, so they stay crisp on any screen.
- **Real text.** Headlines remain text, so search engines, screen readers and translation tools can read them, and the
  words can change without re-rendering anything.
- **Interactive.** A scene can play on load, when it scrolls into view, in step with the scroll, or slide by slide on
  click, and any layer can be a link.
- **Kind to motion-sensitive visitors.** Scenes respect the visitor's reduced-motion setting automatically and show a
  still frame instead.
