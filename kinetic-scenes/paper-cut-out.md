# Paper cut-out explainers

A paper cut-out explainer acts out an idea in a small world made of construction paper: layered shapes with real depth,
a heavy banner for each beat, captions on torn paper tags, and cuts that tear, turn or peel. It suits short explainers,
because a physical metaphor makes an abstract idea easy to follow.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/paper-look.png" alt="A paper world: a sun and layered hills, an orange banner and a caption on a paper tag" width="360" />

## Make one

Open the [start screen](./start-screen.md) and choose **A paper cut-out explainer**.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/start-screen-paper.png" alt="The paper explainer step: what it should explain, format, length and sound, with Start from the template and Create buttons" width="700" />

- **Create** writes the story and builds the whole video. Say what it should explain, choose a format and length, and
  tick **Add a voice-over** and **Add music** if you want them. This needs AI; with your AI agent connected, it builds
  in a new project, step by step.
- **Start from the template** needs no AI. It opens a finished two-part scene, with the sky, sun, hills and camera
  already set, for you to change. You'll also find it under **File › New from template**.

## How the story works

The AI is told to pick one metaphor and stay inside it, such as a post office for email. It opens on a claim or a surprise
in the first three seconds, shows the problem in that world, names the idea, shows how it works in two or three moves,
then shows the result and ends on one clear point. Lines are short, at about 3.5 to 4 words a second, and there's a new
set or a clear change every 2 to 3 seconds.

## The parts

You can use any of these in your own scenes.

### Cut paper

**Cut paper** is a preset, in the Ambient group. Put it on any layer for a hand-cut edge, a fine grain and a shadow that
lifts it off the layer below. Run the clip for the whole layer: the look holds to the end.

| Option | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Rough edge** | How uneven the cut is. Keep it near 1 to 1.5 on anything with words, so the letters stay crisp. | 2.5 |
| **Paper grain** | How much paper texture shows. | 0.3 |
| **Shadow** | How high the sheet sits. Raise it for layers nearer the viewer. | 1 |
| **Re-cuts per second** | Above 0, the edge changes a few times a second, like stop motion. | 0 |

**Torn caption tags:** give a text layer a pale pill, then add **Cut paper** to it.

### Paper cuts

Three new transitions sit with the other **scene transitions**, and work on any two groups.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/paper-cuts.png" alt="Three frames: a ragged paper tear, a page turning, and a strip of tape sweeping across" width="540" />

| Transition | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Paper tear** | The sheet rips across the stage with a pale ragged edge. You can set how ragged it is, and the edge colour. |
| **Page turn** | The scene turns from its left edge with a soft shadow, uncovering the next. |
| **Tape pull** | A strip of masking tape is pulled across, taking the old picture with it. You can set the tape colour. |

### Depth

Give each layer a **Depth** from -1 (far) to 1 (near), then add two camera keys with a slow pan. The layers slide past
each other, which is what makes flat paper feel like a real model.

### Sounds

Four new sounds are in the **Sounds** tab: **Paper rustle**, **Paper tear**, **Tape pull** and **Rubber stamp**. The
paper cuts use their matching sound by default.

### The style

**Paper cut-out** is also a [motion style](./motion-styles.md), so you can apply it to any scene or ask AI assist for it
by name. Its colours are a dusty blue sky with warm orange and teal, and its fonts are Archivo Black and Fraunces.

## Limits

- The world is built from shapes. Detailed props, such as a character or a building, take more layers, or your own
  transparent pictures with **Cut paper** on them.
- **Cut paper** runs a filter on each layer, so a scene with dozens of paper layers can play less smoothly in the preview.
  Exported videos aren't affected.
- Paper styles use the colours you give them. For a brand video, change the palette under **Styles**.
