# Roadmap

Fourteen phases, built in order on one scene file, so nothing made early has to be redone later. All fourteen are built
and the Studio is in beta at [kinetic-scenes.unysonplus.com](https://kinetic-scenes.unysonplus.com/): the editor,
export, the Unyson+ element, AI assist, 3D, sound and voice, fluid motion, product tours, the AI director, video mode,
video editing effects, and the speed-ups and in-browser video export of phase 14. The [guide pages](./index.md) describe
how to use each of them.

## Order of work

1. **Foundations.** The scene file, the player and the responsive stage, because everything else reads or writes them.
2. **Editor.** The canvas, the timeline, the inspector and the presets: enough to build a real hero by hand.
3. **Export.** Standalone HTML and an embed snippet, each recording the player version it was made with.
4. **Unyson+ integration.** The Kinetic Scene element with an **Open in Kinetic Scenes** round trip.
5. **AI.** Prompts produce scene files through the [AI Dev Kit](/ai-dev-kit), so every result can be edited
   on the timeline.
6. **3D and finer control.** three.js layers, a keyframe and curve editor, and scroll-linked playback.
7. **Later.** Video export, click-to-advance presentations and share links.
8. **Sound and voice.** Built-in sound effects, voice-overs with their words, styles, and scenes made from a voice-over.
9. **Fluid motion.** Transitions, the camera, paths, beat sync, captions and the motion that hand-made reels use.
10. **Product tours and interactive scenes.** Links, hover effects, the looping ending, pinning to the stage, cursors,
    focus and screenshots that come alive.
11. **The AI director.** The AI plans with you, builds, checks, looks at rendered frames and fixes what it sees, with
    tools from the kit and only the subscription you already have; plus a quick scene from any website.
12. **Video mode.** Formats, transparent overlays for video editors, and videos that feel like one camera move.

The plan and the reasons behind it are on the [overview](./index.md); what already exists elsewhere is on the
[research](./research.md) page.
