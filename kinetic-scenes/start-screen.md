# The start screen

When you open the Studio, it asks **What do you want to make today?** Every way to begin is on this one screen, so you
don't have to know which menu holds what.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/start-screen.png" alt="The start screen: a Describe it box, the ways to start, a gallery of styles, and on the right your recent work and what's new" width="1000" />

Open it at any time with **File › Start something new**.

## The ways to start

| Start from | What you get | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Describe it** | Type your idea. The AI plans and builds it. | AI assist connected |
| **A style** | Pick any of the 55 styles, then describe your idea or start with a title card. See [Start from a style](#start-from-a-style). | AI assist connected, or nothing for a title card |
| **A website** | A scene from the site's real words, colours, fonts and screenshots. | AI assist connected |
| **A long video** | The best moments, cut into a reel. See [Make a reel](./make-a-reel.md). | The AI Dev Kit |
| **A video you like** | Yours, made with the same structure and pacing, using your own words. Nothing from the video is copied. | The AI Dev Kit |
| **A screen recording** | A product tour from a recording's `video.mp4` and `actions.json`. See [Screencasts](./screencasts.md). | A recording from the AI Dev Kit |
| **A template** | A finished scene to change. See [Templates](./templates.md). | Nothing |
| **An empty scene** | A blank stage to build on yourself. | Nothing |
| **A spreadsheet** | One design, a video for every row. See [Batch videos](./batch-videos.md). | A scene with placeholders in it |
| **A saved scene file** | Opens a scene you downloaded. | Nothing |

If something is missing, the screen says so on that option, for example **Needs the AI Dev Kit** or **Turn on AI in the
kit**. You can still choose it: AI assist shows how to connect, and your request waits there until it can run.

### On a phone

On a small touch screen the start box opens with a note: you can look around and play scenes on a phone, but making
and editing them needs a computer (the editor is made for a mouse and a big screen). Many people find Kinetic Scenes
from an AI chat on their phone; the note tells them to come back on a computer. Making a scene from a chat app:
[ChatGPT, Gemini and other chat apps](./ai-agents.md#chatgpt-gemini-and-other-chat-apps).

## Start from a style

The bottom of the start screen shows seven styles as pictures: your own first, then the ones you used last, then a mix covering the main uses. The eighth tile, **See all styles**, opens the [Style library](./style-library.md) with all 49, searchable and grouped.

- **Hover over a style** to watch it play. It stops when you move away. You can also move to it with the Tab key.
- **Click a style** to choose it. A style chosen in the Style library with **Start something new in it** lands on the same step.

After you choose, you have two ways to go on:

| Choice | What you get | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Create** | Describe your idea, and the AI builds it in that style. | AI assist connected |
| **Start with a title card** | A simple opening scene in that style's colours, fonts and motion, to build on yourself. | Nothing |

**Paper cut-out** opens its own step, with a template and a brief written for that style. See [Paper cut-out explainers](./paper-cut-out.md).

The pictures show what each style can look like. A title card uses the style's look but starts plain, so it won't match its preview until you build on it. The same styles are in the **Styles** tab on the left and in **Scene › Style library…**, where you can also apply one to the scene you're working on.

### Limits

- Your own styles have no photo. They show their colours until you hover, then play their title card.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, previews stay as pictures and don't play.
- On a touch screen there is no hover, so you see the pictures only.

## Describing an idea or giving a website

These two take one more step before anything is made.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/start-screen-website.png" alt="The website step: address, an optional focus, format, length and style, with Back and Create buttons" width="700" />

1. **Say what it's about.** For a website, type its address (`your-product.com` is enough) and, if you like, what to
   focus on.
2. **Choose a format.** **Reel** (9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok), **Video** (16:9), **Square** (1:1) or **Web
   animation** (plays on a page, on every screen size).
3. **Choose a length** of 15 to 60 seconds, or let the AI choose. A web animation has no length to set here.
4. **Choose a style**, or let the AI choose. Your choice also becomes AI assist's style.
5. **Choose sound.** With an AI agent available, tick **Add a voice-over** and **Add music** for a video.
6. **Click Create.**

Nothing is rendered. You preview the scene in the Studio and export it when you're ready.

### With your AI agent

If the AI Dev Kit is running and Claude Code is on your computer, **Create makes a new project and your AI agent builds
the whole video in it.** AI assist opens and shows each step as it happens: reading the website, recording the voice,
writing the scene, looking at its frames and fixing what it finds. The Studio plays each saved version as it arrives.

- **Clicking Create is your one approval.** The agent then makes as many edits as it needs, inside that project's folder
  only. It can't run commands, go on the web or touch anything else.
- **It never renders**, and never touches a site or GitHub.
- **Stop** ends it at any time. What it already saved stays, and **Undo** goes back.
- **A whole video takes about 10 to 20 minutes.** A test reel of 15 seconds, with a voice-over and music, took about 12.
  The work carries on if you close the tab.
- **It uses your Claude subscription.**

A voice-over needs a voice installed in **Tools › Add-ons**. Music is composed for the scene and added at 25% volume.

### Without an agent

AI assist builds the scene here in the browser instead. With its usual settings on, it shows
[three directions, then a plan](./ai.md) for you to approve first.

## Picking up where you left off

The right side lists the scene you have open and your four most recent projects. Click one to open it, or **All
projects** to see the rest. Project folders need the AI Dev Kit; without it, this side says so, and your scene stays in
this browser.

To carry on with the scene in front of you, click its name, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click outside the box.

## Report a bug or ask for a feature

Under the **Done for you** card, **Found a bug or have an idea for a feature? Contact us** opens the
[Unyson+ contact form](https://www.unysonplus.com/contact/?area=Kinetic%20Scenes) with **Kinetic Scenes** already
picked. The same form is in **Help › Report a bug or an idea**, so it is there even when the start screen is
turned off.

- **No account is needed**: give your name, an email for the reply, what it is about (a bug, a feature request, a
  question or something else) and your message. It reaches the Unyson+ team directly; replies usually take a couple of
  days.
- **For a bug**, say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, and which browser you use. If the scene
  matters, mention it: you can send it to us later as a [scene link](./export.md#scene-links-open-a-scene-from-the-web).
- **Have a GitHub account?** You can also open an issue on the
  [Kinetic Scenes repository](https://github.com/UnysonPlus/UnysonPlus-Kinetic-Scenes/issues), where everyone can follow
  the answer and the fix.

## What happens to your current work

- **An open project is never overwritten.** Anything new you start here first closes the project, so its folder stays
  exactly as it was. The new scene is kept in this browser until you save it as a project.
- **A scene that isn't in a project** is replaced, and **Undo** brings it back.
- Choosing **A spreadsheet** keeps your scene, because the spreadsheet fills that design.

## What's new

The start screen lists the newest features under **New**. Click one, or **See what's new**, to open the full list. You can
also open it at any time with **Help › What's new**.

Each feature has a short description and a **Read more** link. Most also have a **Try it** button that opens the feature.

Until you open the list, a dot shows on the **Tools** and **Help** menus and a **New** tag shows beside the new tools. Both
go away once you've opened it, and they come back when there's more news. This is remembered in your browser, so a
different browser or a private window shows them again.

You'll also find these features where you need them:

| Feature | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| [Talking-head effects](./video-effects.md#effects-on-a-person) | The presets library's **Edit a talking head** goal, and the new pieces under **Insert › A piece…**. |
| [Narrated screencasts](./screencasts.md#the-voice-over-and-a-written-guide-from-the-same-take) | In the AI Dev Kit: `narrate`, `voice` and `guide` after a recording, or your AI agent's screencast tools. |
| [Long videos](./long-videos.md) | **Loop** on a selected music clip; the AI Dev Kit's `join` for chapters. |
| [Painted](./painted.md) | **Paint it** on a selected shape or line, and in the Ambient presets. |
| [Build with cursors](./build-with-cursors.md) | In **Tools**. |
| [Second listener](./second-listener.md) | Above **Render video** in **Export**, when the scene has a voice-over. Also on a selected voice clip, and in **Tools**. |
| [Drop with physics](./sketch-and-physics.md#drop-with-physics) | In the Inspector when two or more layers are selected, and in **Tools**. |
| [Hand-drawn](./sketch-and-physics.md#hand-drawn) | **Make it hand-drawn** on a selected shape, and in the Ambient presets. |

## The welcome video

The first time someone opens Kinetic Scenes, a **3-minute video tour** opens in front of the start box. It covers:

- **Why Kinetic Scenes:** your AI picks from over a hundred ready-made animations instead of writing a video from
  scratch (faster, fewer tokens), each project keeps its scene and its notes so your agent picks up where it left off,
  and small fixes are yours in the Studio, with no new render to check.
- **The workspace:** the stage, the timeline, the Inspector, the presets, pieces, sounds and styles, and Export.
- **HTML vs MP4:** the same scene playing live on a web page, with its own phone layout, at a fraction of the size of the
  video.
- **Connecting your AI:** downloading the [AI Dev Kit](/ai-dev-kit), double-clicking `start.bat` (Windows) or
  `start.command` (Mac), using your Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor subscription or an API key, and writing your prompt
  wherever your agent runs (the Code tab in the Claude app, VS Code, Cursor or a terminal).

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/welcome-video.jpg" alt="The welcome video in front of the start box" width="640" />

- **Press play** to watch it with sound. It does not start by itself: a muted autoplay would make YouTube lay its
  automatic captions over the subtitles already in the picture.
- **Start making** (or ×, or a click outside it) closes it and leaves the start box. **Watch on YouTube** opens it there.
- It opens **once per browser**. It does not open for someone who arrives through a project or scene link, or from a
  Unyson+ page editor, since they came for a particular scene.
- To watch it again: **Help › Watch the intro (3 min)**, or **Watch the 3-minute tour** in the start box under the
  done-for-you card, or [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GyLvSuLcbuU).
- The video is a YouTube embed from `youtube-nocookie.com` (YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode), and it loads only while the
  dialog is open.

## Did you know? (tips for returning visitors)

Someone who has been here before (who has seen the welcome video) gets one thing Kinetic Scenes can do each time the
start box opens, under the done-for-you card, in place of the tour link:

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/did-you-know.png" alt="A Did you know? tip in the start box: Export a video or a web page, with Show me, How it works, Another tip, Everything it can do and Hide tips" width="246" />

- **Show me** opens the part of the Studio that does it (the spreadsheet window, Free stock, the 3D device, Export…),
  where there is one. **How it works** opens its page in this manual.
- **Ask your AI:** most tips carry a prompt to copy and give to Claude through the [AI Dev Kit](/ai-dev-kit), or to
  AI assist. Tips marked **AI Dev Kit** need the free kit running on your computer.
- **A video:** a tip whose short has been published shows its thumbnail; clicking it plays the short (a YouTube embed
  from `youtube-nocookie.com`, loaded only while it plays). Tips with a video come up first.
- **Another tip** shows the next one. You don't see a tip again until you have seen them all, then the round starts over.
- **Everything it can do** opens the full list (below). **Hide tips** turns them off for this browser and brings the
  tour link back; Help › What Kinetic Scenes can do still has them all.

## What Kinetic Scenes can do

**Help › What Kinetic Scenes can do** lists everything you can make, by hand or by asking your AI (40 things, from a
voice-over in your own voice to a 3D phone playing your screen recording), grouped as Let the AI make it; Voice, music
and sound; People on camera; Pictures, footage and 3D; Looks and styles; Products and websites; Finish and share.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/what-it-can-do.png" alt="The What Kinetic Scenes can do window: a search box, then each thing with what it is, a prompt to copy, Show me and How it works" width="560" />

- **Search** by any word ("voice", "3D", "captions", "website").
- Each entry has what it is, **Copy** for its prompt, **Show me** and **How it works**, and its short once it is
  published. It is the list to open when you wonder whether Kinetic Scenes can do something.
- The individual effects, pieces and styles are in their own libraries (the [presets](./presets.md), the
  [pieces](./video-effects.md#ready-made-pieces) and the [55 styles](./motion-styles.md)); this list is the things
  people want to make.

## When it opens

It opens each time the Studio does, with two exceptions, so it never gets in the way of a job you came to do:

- You arrived from a project link, such as the one your AI agent gives you.
- You came from a Unyson+ page editor with **Open in Kinetic Scenes**.

To stop it opening, clear **Show this when the Studio opens**. The choice is kept in this browser. **File › Start
something new** still opens it.

## Your first scene

The first time you make a scene from a template or an empty stage, the [first-scene guide](./templates.md#your-first-scene-in-two-minutes)
appears in the corner and continues from **Change the words**.

## Limits

- The format, length and style step is only for **Describe it** and **A website**. A long video and a video you like
  ask for their own settings in their own dialogs, and the other options start straight away.
- **Add a voice-over** and **Add music** need the agent, and only appear for a video. Without it, add sound afterwards
  with [Sound](./sound.md).
- The agent skips the three directions and the plan: it builds from what you asked for. Change the result by asking
  in AI assist.
