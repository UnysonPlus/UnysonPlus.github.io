# The start screen

When you open the Studio, it asks **What do you want to make today?** Every way to begin is on this one screen, so you
don't have to know which menu holds what.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/start-screen.png" alt="The start screen: a Describe it box, the ways to start, a gallery of styles, and on the right your recent work and what's new" width="1000" />

Open it at any time with **File › Start something new**.

## The ways to start

| Start from | What you get | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Describe it** | Type your idea. The AI plans and builds it. | AI assist connected |
| **A style** | Pick any of the 30 styles, then describe your idea or start with a title card. See [Start from a style](#start-from-a-style). | AI assist connected, or nothing for a title card |
| **A website** | A scene from the site's real words, colours, fonts and screenshots. | AI assist connected |
| **A long video** | The best moments, cut into a reel. See [Make a reel](./make-a-reel.md). | The AI Dev Kit |
| **A video you like** | Yours, made with the same structure and pacing, using your own words. Nothing from the video is copied. | The AI Dev Kit |
| **A screen recording** | A product tour from a recording's `video.mp4` and `actions.json`. See [Screencasts](./screencasts.md). | A recording from the AI Dev Kit |
| **A template** | A finished scene to change. See [Templates](./templates.md). | Nothing |
| **An empty scene** | A blank stage to build on yourself. | Nothing |
| **A spreadsheet** | One design, a video for every row. See [Batch videos](./batch-videos.md). | A scene with placeholders in it |
| **A saved scene file** | Opens a scene you downloaded. | Nothing |

If something is missing, the screen says so on that option, for example **Needs the AI Dev Kit** or **Turn on AI in the
kit**. You can still choose it: AI assist shows how to connect, and your request waits there until it can run.

## Start from a style

The bottom of the start screen shows every style as a picture, so you can see what each one looks like before you choose. The screen scrolls, and the header and your recent work stay in place.

- **Hover over a style** to watch it play. It stops when you move away. You can also move to it with the Tab key.
- **Use the filters** to narrow the list: **Tech and product**, **Clean and bold**, **Drawn and crafted** and **Retro and playful**. **Yours** appears when you have styles of your own.
- **Click a style** to choose it.

After you choose, you have two ways to go on:

| Choice | What you get | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Create** | Describe your idea, and the AI builds it in that style. | AI assist connected |
| **Start with a title card** | A simple opening scene in that style's colours, fonts and motion, to build on yourself. | Nothing |

**Paper cut-out** opens its own step, with a template and a brief written for that style. See [Paper cut-out explainers](./paper-cut-out.md).

The pictures show what each style can look like. A title card uses the style's look but starts plain, so it won't match its preview until you build on it. The same styles are in the **Styles** tab on the left, where you can also apply one to the scene you're working on.

### Limits

- Your own styles have no photo. They show their colours until you hover, then play their title card.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, previews stay as pictures and don't play.
- On a touch screen there is no hover, so you see the pictures only.

## Describing an idea or giving a website

These two take one more step before anything is made.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/start-screen-website.png" alt="The website step: address, an optional focus, format, length and style, with Back and Create buttons" width="700" />

1. **Say what it's about.** For a website, type its address (`your-product.com` is enough) and, if you like, what to
   focus on.
2. **Choose a format.** **Reel** (9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok), **Video** (16:9), **Square** (1:1) or **Web
   animation** (plays on a page, on every screen size).
3. **Choose a length** of 15 to 60 seconds, or let the AI choose. A web animation has no length to set here.
4. **Choose a style**, or let the AI choose. Your choice also becomes AI assist's style.
5. **Choose sound.** With an AI agent available, tick **Add a voice-over** and **Add music** for a video.
6. **Click Create.**

Nothing is rendered. You preview the scene in the Studio and export it when you're ready.

### With your AI agent

If the AI Dev Kit is running and Claude Code is on your computer, **Create makes a new project and your AI agent builds
the whole video in it.** AI assist opens and shows each step as it happens: reading the website, recording the voice,
writing the scene, looking at its frames and fixing what it finds. The Studio plays each saved version as it arrives.

- **Clicking Create is your one approval.** The agent then makes as many edits as it needs, inside that project's folder
  only. It can't run commands, go on the web or touch anything else.
- **It never renders**, and never touches a site or GitHub.
- **Stop** ends it at any time. What it already saved stays, and **Undo** goes back.
- **A whole video takes about 10 to 20 minutes.** A test reel of 15 seconds, with a voice-over and music, took about 12.
  The work carries on if you close the tab.
- **It uses your Claude subscription.**

A voice-over needs a voice installed in **Tools › Add-ons**. Music is composed for the scene and added at 25% volume.

### Without an agent

AI assist builds the scene here in the browser instead. With its usual settings on, it shows
[three directions, then a plan](./ai.md) for you to approve first.

## Picking up where you left off

The right side lists the scene you have open and your four most recent projects. Click one to open it, or **All
projects** to see the rest. Project folders need the AI Dev Kit; without it, this side says so, and your scene stays in
this browser.

To carry on with the scene in front of you, click its name, press <kbd>Esc</kbd> or click outside the box.

## What happens to your current work

- **An open project is never overwritten.** Anything new you start here first closes the project, so its folder stays
  exactly as it was. The new scene is kept in this browser until you save it as a project.
- **A scene that isn't in a project** is replaced, and **Undo** brings it back.
- Choosing **A spreadsheet** keeps your scene, because the spreadsheet fills that design.

## What's new

The start screen lists the newest features under **New**. Click one, or **See what's new**, to open the full list. You can
also open it at any time with **Help › What's new**.

Each feature has a short description and a **Read more** link. Most also have a **Try it** button that opens the feature.

Until you open the list, a dot shows on the **Tools** and **Help** menus and a **New** tag shows beside the new tools. Both
go away once you've opened it, and they come back when there's more news. This is remembered in your browser, so a
different browser or a private window shows them again.

You'll also find these features where you need them:

| Feature | Where it shows up |
|---|---|
| [Build with cursors](./build-with-cursors.md) | In **Tools**. |
| [Second listener](./second-listener.md) | Above **Render video** in **Export**, when the scene has a voice-over. Also on a selected voice clip, and in **Tools**. |
| [Drop with physics](./sketch-and-physics.md#drop-with-physics) | In the Inspector when two or more layers are selected, and in **Tools**. |
| [Hand-drawn](./sketch-and-physics.md#hand-drawn) | **Make it hand-drawn** on a selected shape, and in the Ambient presets. |

## When it opens

It opens each time the Studio does, with two exceptions, so it never gets in the way of a job you came to do:

- You arrived from a project link, such as the one your AI agent gives you.
- You came from a Unyson+ page editor with **Open in Kinetic Scenes**.

To stop it opening, clear **Show this when the Studio opens**. The choice is kept in this browser. **File › Start
something new** still opens it.

## Your first scene

The first time you make a scene from a template or an empty stage, the [first-scene guide](./templates.md#your-first-scene-in-two-minutes)
appears in the corner and continues from **Change the words**.

## Limits

- The format, length and style step is only for **Describe it** and **A website**. A long video and a video you like
  ask for their own settings in their own dialogs, and the other options start straight away.
- **Add a voice-over** and **Add music** need the agent, and only appear for a video. Without it, add sound afterwards
  with [Sound](./sound.md).
- The agent skips the three directions and the plan: it builds from what you asked for. Change the result by asking
  in AI assist.
