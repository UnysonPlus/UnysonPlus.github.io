# Style library

Kinetic Scenes has 49 styles, from clean product looks to chalkboards, newsreels, ukiyo-e prints and thermal cameras.
The **Style library** shows them all in one window, large enough to choose a look by eye.

<img src="/img/kinetic-scenes/style-library.png" alt="The Style library window: a search box, the group filters with Drawn and crafted selected, a grid of styles, and the Whiteboard style shown large on the right with its description and the buttons Start something new in it, Use with AI assist and Export" width="1180" />

## Open it

- **Scene › Style library…** in the menu.
- **See all styles** (the last tile) or **Style library** (top right) under **Start from a style** on the
  [start screen](./start-screen.md#start-from-a-style).
- **Browse all styles** at the top of the **Styles** tab on the left.

## Find a style

- **Search** with any word: a style's name, what it looks like, its fonts or its group. Every word must match, so
  *retro film* finds Newsreel, Rubber-hose cartoon and Silent film, and *chalk* finds Chalkboard.
- **Filter** by group: **Tech and product**, **Clean and bold**, **Drawn and crafted** and **Retro and playful**.
  **Yours** appears when you have styles of your own (brand kits, imported or saved styles).
- **Hover** over a style to watch it play. With the keyboard, Tab to it.

## Pick a style

Click a style to see it large and playing, with:

- what it is in one line, and what makes it recognisable (the same guide AI assist follows);
- its colours and fonts.

Then choose what to do with it:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start something new in it** | Opens the start screen's next step for that style: describe your idea and AI assist builds it in the style, or start with a title card in it. |
| **Apply to this scene** | Restyles the scene you have: tick **Colours and fonts**, **Motion** and **Sounds** for what to change. Undo brings back the previous look. Shown when a scene is open. |
| **Use with AI assist** | AI assist follows the style whenever it makes or changes a scene. Click again to stop. |
| **Export** | Downloads the style as a file to share; **Import a style** reads it. |
| **Edit**, **Delete** | For your own styles only. |

With no style picked, the right side offers **Create a brand kit** (your colours, fonts and logo as a style) and
**Import a style**. To turn the scene you made into a style, use **Save this scene as a style** in the **Styles** tab.

## On the start screen

The start screen shows seven styles, with the eighth tile opening the library:

1. **Your own styles** first (up to two).
2. **The styles you used last**: picked, applied or given to AI assist.
3. **A fixed mix** covering the main uses: Living UI, Clean, Whiteboard, Paper cut-out, Data story, Vertical social,
   Newsreel and Neon sign.

What you used is remembered in this browser, so another browser or a private window starts with the mix.

## Limits

- Your own styles have no photo: they show their colours until you hover, then play their title card.
- With reduce motion turned on in your device settings, the tiles stay still; the picked style still plays.
- On a phone, the picked style appears under the grid, and the window scrolls to it.
