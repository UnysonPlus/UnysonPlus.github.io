# Templates and your first scene

You don't have to start from an empty stage. **File › New from template** opens finished scenes chosen by what you want
to make, built in the style you pick. Change the words and pictures and it's ready; or ask AI assist to build on it.

## Your first scene in two minutes

The first time you open the Studio, a small guide in the corner walks you through it. Each step outlines the part of
the Studio it's about:

1. **Pick a starting point:** choose a template.
2. **Change the words:** click any text on the stage and type your own in the Inspector. Pictures change the same way.
3. **Watch it play:** press play or the Space bar. The timeline shows when each part arrives.
4. **Export it:** plain HTML for a web page, or a video for Reels, TikTok, Shorts or YouTube.

It shows once per browser. **Help › Your first scene (guide)** opens it again.

## The templates

| Template | For | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Website hero** | Introducing a product on a home page: a kicker, a big headline, a line and a button, looping calmly. Has a phone layout too. | Web, 16:9 and 4:5 |
| **Product reveal** | The product slides in inside a browser frame while three benefits land beside it. | Video 16:9 |
| **Feature list** | A title and four features, each with a mark, arriving one by one. | Video 16:9 |
| **Pricing** | Three plan cards; the prices count up and the recommended plan gets a badge. | Video 16:9 |
| **Call to action** | One strong line, a button that invites the click, a held end frame. | Video 16:9 |
| **Logo intro** | A mark, the name opening out beside it, a tagline under them. | Video 16:9 |
| **Social promo** | A Reel, Short or TikTok: a hook in the first second, three quick beats, a call to action, all clear of the app's buttons. | Video 9:16 |
| **Explainer** | Longer pieces in chapters: chapter cards, a chapter progress bar, a "Chapter 1 of 3" label and subtitles. Replace the script, or ask AI assist to fill it from yours. | Video 16:9 |
| **Code demo** | A code window types a snippet, the key line gets a note, and it runs. | Video 16:9 |

Every template works in every style, from the original looks to the [motion styles](./motion-styles.md), and follows the
film's shape AI assist uses: something happens at once, one hero moment, and a held end frame. All of them pass Check
scene with no warnings. The words are placeholders written to be replaced, and the product shot is a placeholder picture.

## Presets by goal

The Presets tab can also be browsed **by goal** instead of by technique: *Make a headline land*, *Reveal a picture*,
*Draw attention*, *Change scenes*, *Show numbers and data*, *Keep a frame alive*, *Show a product or app*, *Give it a
look* and *Explain code*. Each goal has a short hint and the presets that do it, best first. Switch with **By goal / By
technique** above the list; the Studio remembers your choice.
