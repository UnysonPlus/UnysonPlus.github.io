# SEO Basics

**SEO (search engine optimization) is helping search engines understand your pages so the
right people find them.** Most of it is just… making a good, clear page. There are no magic
tricks — there are fundamentals.

## The fundamentals

- **A unique title & meta description per page.** The `<title>` is the clickable headline in
  Google; the description is the snippet under it. Make each page's own and descriptive.
- **One clear topic per page.** A page that answers one question well beats a page that
  half-answers ten.
- **Good headings.** Your heading structure tells search engines what the page is about
  (see [Semantic HTML](./semantic-html)).
- **Descriptive URLs.** `/blue-running-shoes` beats `/product?id=8472`.
- **Alt text.** It helps image search and accessibility at once.
- **Structured data** (schema.org) can earn *rich results* — star ratings, breadcrumbs,
  FAQs — but only mark up things genuinely on the page; faking it gets sites penalised.
- **Write for humans.** Google rewards content that actually helps; keyword-stuffing is
  penalised, not rewarded.
