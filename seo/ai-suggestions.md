# AI suggestions and AI answers

These use the AI connection set up for the [AI Assistant](./ai-assistant.md): **your account, your
usage**, no credits to buy. Without a connection they don't appear.

## Suggestions in the SEO box

Each page's **SEO** box has **Suggest with AI:** buttons:

| Button | You get |
| --- | --- |
| **Titles** | Three SEO titles under 60 characters. |
| **Descriptions** | Three meta descriptions of 120–155 characters. |
| **FAQ questions** | Four questions a reader would ask, each with a short answer taken from the page. Paste them into an [FAQ accordion](./structured-data.md#faq-data). |
| **Key points** | The page's five key points. |

They are written **from the page as last saved**, use its [focus keyphrase](./content-analysis.md)
naturally if it has one, are told never to invent facts or prices the page doesn't state, and come with a
**Copy** button. Nothing is written into the page: you paste what you want.

**Sound like your site.** Under **SEO → General → AI suggestions**, set a **Tone** ("warm and
plain-spoken, no hype") and an **Audience** ("families planning a seaside weekend"); every suggestion is
asked for in that voice.

## AI answers: does an assistant mention you?

Which sites an AI assistant names when someone asks it a question is becoming a real way people find
businesses. **SEO → AI answers** samples it:

1. List the questions your customers ask, one per line (up to 20): *Where can I get a good crab linguine
   in Harbourtown?*
2. Save, then press **Ask the questions now**.

Each question is put to your AI connection as a member of the public would ask it. The result is stored
as a **dated sample**: for each question, whether the answer mentioned your site (its domain, or its name
as whole words), with the answer itself to read.

**Read it as a sample, not a ranking.** Answers vary between runs, between people and between model
versions, and the assistant you connected is not every assistant people use. A row that says "mentioned"
means it was mentioned in that answer, that day. That is why it only runs when you press the button, and
why it is never turned into a position or a score. Each question is one paid call on your account.
