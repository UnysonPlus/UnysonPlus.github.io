# Search performance

**SEO → Search performance** shows how each page is doing in Google over the last four weeks, from your
own [Search Console](./search-console.md) data, and, more usefully, **how that changed** against the
four weeks before.

| Column | |
| --- | --- |
| **Clicks** | Visits from Google, with the change (+12 in green, −8 in red). |
| **Impressions** | How often the page appeared in results, with the change. |
| **Click-through** | Clicks per hundred impressions. |
| **Position** | The page's average position, with the change: a positive number means it moved **up**. |

Three lists, 25 pages each:

- **Gaining**: more clicks or impressions than before.
- **Slipping**: fewer. Before changing a page, look at [Topic momentum](./topic-momentum.md): if interest
  in the topic itself fell, the page is not the problem.
- **Seen but not clicked**: at least 200 impressions and fewer than 1 click in 100. The page ranks; its
  title and description don't make people choose it, and those are what the page's SEO box edits.

Each page name opens its editor.

## Limits

- Pages with fewer than 50 impressions in both windows are left out: at that size a change is noise.
- Google publishes Search Console data two to three days late, and the figures are kept for six hours.
- Needs Search Console connected; without it the screen says so.
